# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Pulse a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, appearance, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's sensitive information.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Prioritize the health of the community and the safety of Pulse users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or sexualized attention
- Trolling, insulting comments, or sustained disruption
- Publishing private information without explicit permission
- Deliberate attempts to weaken project or user security
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the Petauron organization owners through their published GitHub contact methods. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.

Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
